## 4. Site architecture (what's built)

```
/                                   Home — positioning, services, roadmap, work, areas, guides, FAQ
/startup-launch-package             Ads landing page with lead form (Google/Meta Ads destination)
/services/website-development
/services/app-development
/services/social-media-marketing
/services/seo-services
/services/digital-marketing         (Meta & Google Ads)
/locations/bangalore
/locations/shivamogga
/blog  +  /blog/:slug               8 posts
/privacy-policy  /terms             Required by Meta lead ads & Google Ads policies
/thank-you                          Conversion URL (noindex, disallowed in robots.txt)
```

Old URLs `/services/custom-software-development`, `/business-automation` and `/ai-solutions` redirect to `/services/app-development` (301 in `public/_redirects`, plus a client-side redirect). The three retired pre-pivot posts redirect to `/blog`.

**Technical SEO in place**
- Every marketing route is **prerendered to static HTML** at build time (`scripts/build.mjs`), so crawlers and Meta/WhatsApp/LinkedIn link previews get full content and per-page title, description, canonical and OG tags without running JS.
- Schema.org JSON-LD: `ProfessionalService` (areaServed Bangalore + Shivamogga) on every page, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList` and `BlogPosting` where relevant.
- One `<h1>` per page, keyword-led titles, `en-IN` language, geo meta.
- Branded 1200×630 OG images per service, location and post (`npm run og-images`).
- `sitemap.xml` generated from the route list on every build (also written to `public/sitemap.xml`).
- Real 404s (no soft-404 homepage fallback) on Netlify/Cloudflare via `_redirects`.
- Heavy legacy pages lazy-loaded; the unused PDF/dashboard app was removed (main bundle ~200 KB gzipped).

## 5. AI assistant (LLM) visibility

Goal: get cited when people ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews things like *"best digital marketing agency for startups in Shivamogga"*.

**Built into the site**
- Prerendered HTML: most AI crawlers don't run JavaScript, so this is the foundation.
- `/llms.txt` (index) and `/llms-full.txt` (all services, locations, FAQs and guides as Markdown), regenerated on every build.
- `/about` page with plain factual statements (who, what, where, founder) and `Organization`, `Person`, `WebSite` and `AboutPage` schema.
- "Key takeaways" at the top of every guide, plus FAQ sections with `FAQPage` schema: short, quotable answers.
- `robots.txt` explicitly allows OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot, bingbot (which feeds Copilot and ChatGPT search), meta-externalagent and DuckAssistBot.

**Things to do outside the site**
- **Cloudflare:** check **Security → Bots** and **AI Crawl Control**. Cloudflare can block AI crawlers by default, which overrides robots.txt. Allow the bots you want.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools:** submit the sitemap. ChatGPT search and Copilot draw heavily on Bing's index.
- **Get mentioned elsewhere:** AI answers favour businesses named on other sites, such as Google Business Profile, Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Justdial, a LinkedIn company page, local news and client sites crediting you. Keep the name, description and contact details identical everywhere.
- **Reviews:** Google reviews that mention the service and city ("built our website in Shivamogga") help both local SEO and AI answers.
- **Test monthly:** ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini "digital marketing agency for startups in Shivamogga" and similar questions, and note whether and how Wbzard Labs appears.

## 6. Google Search Console — submission steps

1. Deploy the new build (`npm run build`, then publish `dist/`).
2. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console) → **Add property** → choose **Domain** → `wbzard.com` → verify with the DNS TXT record at your domain registrar.
   _(Alternative: URL-prefix property + HTML tag. Paste the token into `site.verification.google` in `src/config/site.js` and rebuild.)_
3. **Sitemaps** → submit `https://www.wbzard.com/sitemap.xml`.
4. **URL Inspection** → request indexing for the priority pages, in this order: `/`, `/locations/shivamogga`, `/locations/bangalore`, `/startup-launch-package`, then the five service pages.
5. Check `https://www.wbzard.com/robots.txt` loads and `https://www.wbzard.com/thank-you` shows as "Excluded by noindex" (expected).
6. Repeat for **Bing Webmaster Tools** (you can import directly from Search Console).
7. After 2–4 weeks: review **Performance → Queries** and feed real queries back into page copy and new posts.

## 7. Google Business Profile (do this first, it's free)

- If you have a physical address in Shivamogga or Bangalore, create a profile there. If you work from home or remotely, set it up as a **service-area business** (hide the address, serve Shivamogga + Bangalore).
- Category: **Internet marketing service** (primary), with **Website designer** and **Marketing agency** as secondary.
- Use the same name, email, phone and website as the site (NAP consistency).
- Add the phone number to `site.phone` in `src/config/site.js` so it appears in the footer and schema markup.

## 8. Meta & Google Ads readiness checklist

All of these are wired into the code; they only need IDs in `src/config/site.js`:

| Setting | Where to get it | Field |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads conversion ID + label | Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → New → Website → "Submit lead form" | `tracking.googleAdsId`, `tracking.googleAdsLeadLabel` |
| Meta Pixel ID | Meta Events Manager → Data sources → Pixel | `tracking.metaPixelId` |
| Meta domain verification | Meta Business Settings → Brand safety → Domains | `verification.meta` |
| Lead form endpoint | Formspree / Web3Forms / Getform, or your own API (POST JSON) | `leadFormEndpoint` |
| WhatsApp number | Your WhatsApp Business number, digits only with country code | `whatsapp` |

**Events fired automatically**
- `generate_lead` (GA4), Google Ads conversion, Meta `Lead`: on lead-form submit and on Cal.com booking (`Schedule` is also sent for bookings)
- `contact` / Meta `Contact`: on WhatsApp, phone and email clicks
- `book_call_click`: on any "Book a call" button
- Page views on every route change (GA4 + Meta)
- UTM parameters and `gclid`/`fbclid` are captured on landing and attached to the lead submission

**Recommended first campaigns**
- **Shivamogga:** Meta click-to-WhatsApp ads, 15–25 km radius, creative = "New business? Get online in one plan". Plus Google Search on the Cluster A keywords → `/locations/shivamogga` or `/startup-launch-package`.
- **Bangalore:** Google Search on long-tail intent ("website for startup bangalore", "mvp development bangalore") → service pages. Meta lead ads targeting founders/small business owners → `/startup-launch-package`.
- Use **exact/phrase match** and a negative list (jobs, course, training, free, internship, salary, template).
